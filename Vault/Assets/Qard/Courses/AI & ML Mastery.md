AI Engineering builds applications with pretrained models; **ML** remains one group for training pipelines, NLP representations, prediction metrics, calibration, drift, and fine-tuning.

In Qard Learn's course map, select a group beginning **AI /** to open that subtopic. Start with **LLM foundations**, then **Prompts and context**, or use **Governance and automation** as the entry point for action authority and responsible AI controls. Prerequisites connect the AI Engineering groups where needed. No prerequisite links connect them to ML in either direction; ML starts independently with training and learning signals.
