**Current focus: AI Engineering.** Its groups cover LLM foundations, prompts and context, LLM evaluation, model selection and caching, RAG, tools and agents, coding agents, agent evaluation, and safety. **Machine Learning** is one group for training pipelines, learning signals, NLP, prediction metrics, calibration, drift, and automation levels.

The AI route has one starting point: the **Intro** of LLM foundations. Each group that mirrors a folder starts with an **Intro** objective for that folder's hub note, and every other objective in the group opens only after it. Groups are chained: LLM foundations, then Prompts and context, then LLM evaluation and Model selection and caching; RAG and Tools and agents both follow **Context Engineering**; Coding agents, Agent evaluation and Safety follow Tools and agents.

**Machine Learning** starts separately with its own Intro. No prerequisite links connect it to the AI groups.

Qard groups are flat topic views. Course-wide recommendations and due reviews still include AI Engineering and ML; selecting a group does not pause other reviews or create a separate schedule. Select an objective and use Teach or Route here to follow its prerequisite path.
