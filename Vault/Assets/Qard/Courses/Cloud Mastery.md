**Fundamentals** starts with an **Intro** objective for the Cloud hub note; it is the course's one starting point. The Intro opens the service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, CaaS), which establish what the provider operates and what stays with the application team. The service models open provider fit, cost management and disaster recovery.

Assessment states, due dates and evidence come from study.
