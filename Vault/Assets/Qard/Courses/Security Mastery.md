The course has one starting point: the **Intro** of Fundamentals. Each group starts with an **Intro** objective for its hub note, and every other objective in the group opens only after it.

Inside a group the route is a tree: each objective has one prerequisite in the group, the topic it builds on most directly.

- **Fundamentals:** the Intro opens sensitive data, hashing, authorization models, web vulnerabilities and firewalls. Hashing opens password storage and encryption; encryption opens digital signatures and secrets management; signatures open blockchain; web vulnerabilities open the OWASP categories.
- **Authentication:** opens after digital signatures. Its Intro opens Basic authentication, two-factor authentication (then TOTP), OAuth and OpenID Connect (then JWT bearer validation and single sign-on), and resource-based authorization, which is built on the authorization models from Fundamentals.

Authentication and authorization keep different responsibilities. Existing assessment states and evidence remain intact.
