The course has one starting point: the **Intro** of Fundamentals. The groups run in route order: **Fundamentals → Transport & Sockets → Protocols → Architecture & Ops**. A group with a hub note starts with an **Intro** objective for it, and every other objective in the group opens only after it.

Inside a group the route is a tree: each objective has one prerequisite in the group, the topic it builds on most directly. Transport & Sockets goes from TCP/IP to UDP and to sockets and the host data path. Protocols goes from ports and URLs to HTTP, which opens HTTP/2, REST, WebSockets and live streaming; REST opens GraphQL and RPC, and RPC opens gRPC; DNS opens SMTP. Architecture & Ops starts from forward and reverse proxies, which open NGINX, CDNs and VPNs.

An objective links to another group only where it is built on one thing taught there: HTTP/2 on TCP, WebSockets on sockets, DNS on UDP, CDNs on DNS.

Learning objectives follow the linked source material. Their prerequisite links identify the knowledge needed for each target; the suggested route is guidance rather than a requirement to finish every earlier group. Teach, checks, and reviews retain their existing assessment history. Source-note lifecycle status does not establish course mastery. Maintain the explicit source links in this internal course record.
