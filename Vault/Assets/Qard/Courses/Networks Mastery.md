Start with **OSI model and TCP/IP layers** in **Fundamentals**. **Transport & Sockets** then traces TCP/IP, UDP, socket framing, and the host data path. **Protocols** builds from ports, URLs, and DNS into HTTP, APIs, and specialized application protocols. **Architecture & Ops** applies those mechanisms to proxies, NGINX, CDNs, VPNs, and peer-to-peer systems.

The group order keeps infrastructure and protocol topics easy to find. The starting route crosses those groups: layers → TCP/IP and UDP → sockets or DNS and HTTP → application protocols and operational architecture. Each group lists its own foundations before dependent objectives; an objective can name a prerequisite from another group.

Learning objectives follow the linked source material. Their prerequisite links identify the knowledge needed for each target; the suggested route is guidance rather than a requirement to finish every earlier group. Teach, checks, and reviews retain their existing assessment history. Source-note lifecycle status does not establish course mastery. Maintain the explicit source links in this internal course record.
